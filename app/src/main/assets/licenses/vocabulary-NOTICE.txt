The app ships normalized single-word entries from C3H3-AI/Vocab WordBank.
https://github.com/C3H3-AI/vocab-wordbank
See vocabulary-wordbank-MIT.txt for its MIT license.

CET-4, CET-6 and the supplementary senior list come from KyleBing/english-vocabulary.
https://github.com/KyleBing/english-vocabulary
See vocabulary-kylebing-BSD.txt for its BSD 3-Clause license.

Vocab WordBank attributes its Gaokao 3500 list to lilinji/English.
https://github.com/lilinji/English

The APK keeps English headwords, level tags, and Chinese definitions for CET
entries. It filters out phrases and uses the two school lists as exclusions.
